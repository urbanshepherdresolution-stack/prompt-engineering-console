# 🧠 Prompt Engineering Console

An interactive tool for building optimized prompts with 100+ reasoning modes, cognitive strategies, and problem-solving frameworks.

## ✨ Features

- **100+ Prompt Modes** organized into categories:
  - Modes (Truthmode, ELI10, Red Team, etc.)
  - Reasoning Frameworks (Chain of Thought, ReAct, etc.)
  - Epistemic Modes (Bayesian, Falsifiability, etc.)
  - Problem-Solving (First Principles, Inversion, etc.)
  - Cognitive Strategies (Metacognition, Framing, etc.)
  - Tactical & Human (Execution, Strategic, etc.)
  - Human Factors (Simplicity, Clarity, etc.)
  - Deep Cuts (Ground Truth, Citation, etc.)

- **Interactive Selection** - Click any tag to add/remove modes
- **Live Prompt Generation** - Automatically builds your prompt
- **Copy to Clipboard** - One-click copy
- **Save/Load Configurations** - Persist your favorite setups
- **Select/Deselect All** - Quick bulk actions

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Open `index.html` in your browser
2. Click on any mode tags to build your prompt
3. The generated prompt appears in the console
4. Click "Copy Prompt" to use it

## 💡 How to Use

1. **Select modes** - Click on tags to add them to your prompt
2. **Build your prompt** - The console automatically updates
3. **Generate** - Click "Generate Prompt" to refresh
4. **Copy** - Use the copy button to grab your prompt
5. **Save** - Save your configuration for later
